# Governance

Aura is a small open-source project under the MIT license.

- **Maintainer:** [@balmacefa](https://github.com/balmacefa) has final say on direction, releases and merges (benevolent-maintainer model).
- **Decisions:** discussed in issues and pull requests. Larger changes need an issue first. Lazy consensus: if nobody objects within a reasonable time, the maintainer decides.
- **Contributors:** anyone may propose changes under the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). Consistent, high-quality contributors may be invited as committers; this is at the maintainer's discretion.
- **Releases:** semantic versioning, tagged by the maintainer, with a [changelog](CHANGELOG.md) and published SHA-256 checksums.
- **Principles:** no telemetry, no ads, the core stays free; paid extras (see [docs/MONETIZATION.md](docs/MONETIZATION.md)) never remove existing free features.
- **Succession:** if the maintainer becomes inactive for 6 months, the community may fork under the same license.
